屏幕与输入

应用通过 USB 或同一本地网络，在您自己的 Mac 与 iPhone/iPad 之间传输屏幕帧、触控、滚动、主动发送的文字及连接元数据，以显示和控制 Mac。应用不把屏幕录像保存到文件，也不把画面或输入文字上传开发者服务器。Mac 上接收输入的其他程序如何处理文字，适用该程序的规则。

屏幕录制权限用于采集选定桌面；辅助功能权限用于模拟用户操作并识别可编辑控件的类型和位置，自动键盘不读取控件已有正文。本地网络权限用于发现、配对与连接。接收端需解锁并在前台。

本地保存与安全

无线配对密钥保存在系统钥匙串，不经 iCloud 同步；设备名称、安装标识和协议能力用于连接。语言、画质及显示设置保存在本机。输入草稿保留在运行内存，发送成功后清空。复制配对码时应用写入剪贴板，不主动读取剪贴板内容。无线传输使用本地配对加密，无云中转。

本地诊断日志包含连接状态、设备/服务名称、画面尺寸和错误，不刻意记录输入正文或配对密钥，不自动传给开发者。向支持人员提供日志或截屏前，请先检查并移除无关个人信息。

购买和试用

购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，应用在设备上验证权益。应用不接触银行卡、支付密码或 Apple 账户密码，也没有开发者交易服务器。试用以 Apple 签名的首次领取时间开始连续计时；钥匙串保存交易标识、开始时间、已消耗时长和本地时钟检查值。系统启动时间仅用于本地 elapsed-time 计算，不上传开发者。退出、后台或断开连接仍计时，重新安装或恢复购买不会重新获得试用；试用不自动扣费。Apple 对购买与系统账户的处理适用 Apple 自身政策。

系统听写与其他服务

语音听写属于系统键盘，应用只接收转写后的文字，不采集或保存麦克风音频；可用语言和处理方式受系统听写设置及 Apple 政策约束。应用没有广告、分析 SDK、跟踪或应用账号注册系统。

您的选择与联系支持

可停止接收、关闭无线、更新配对码，或在系统设置撤销屏幕录制、辅助功能和本地网络权限。卸载会删除应用容器，但钥匙串条目可能保留；不能把卸载视为清空全部钥匙串信息或重新获得试用。Mac 的登录后自动启动可在应用或系统登录项中关闭。

如自愿发送支持邮件，公司会收到邮件内容和您提供的联系资料，用于处理请求。请勿发送密码、支付凭据或不相关的桌面内容。网站服务器可能保留提供页面及运维安全所需的常规访问日志；本产品静态页面不加载广告或分析脚本。政策与功能发生变化时会更新本说明。

Screen and input sharing

Screen frames, touch, scrolling, explicitly sent text and connection metadata travel over USB or your local network between your own Mac and iPhone/iPad. They enable display and control. The app does not save screen-video recordings to files or upload screen/input content to a developer server. Other Mac apps receiving your text follow their own privacy practices.

Mac Screen Recording permission captures the selected desktop. Accessibility enables input and detection of editable controls by role and bounds; automatic keyboard detection does not extract existing field text. Local Network permission enables discovery and pairing. Keep the receiver unlocked and foregrounded.

Local storage and security

Pairing secrets stay in the system Keychain without iCloud sync. Device/service names, an installation identifier and protocol capabilities support connections. Preferences remain on-device. Keyboard drafts remain in running-app memory and clear after successful sending. Copy Pairing Code writes to the clipboard; the app does not proactively read it. Wireless sharing uses local pairing encryption and no cloud relay.

Local diagnostic logs contain connection states, device/service names, dimensions and errors. They do not intentionally include typed text or pairing secrets and are not automatically uploaded. Review and remove unrelated personal information before voluntarily sharing diagnostics or screenshots with support.

Purchases and trial

Apple StoreKit processes purchases; the app verifies entitlements locally. It does not access payment-card details, payment passwords or Apple Account passwords, and has no developer transaction server. Trial timing starts at the signed original Apple claim date. Its identifier, start, consumed time and local-clock checkpoints stay in the device Keychain. System uptime is used only for local elapsed-time calculation and is not uploaded. Background time, quitting and disconnecting count; reinstalling or restoring does not reset the trial. There is no automatic charge. Apple's purchase and account processing follows Apple's own policies.

Dictation and other services

Dictation belongs to the system keyboard. The app receives transcribed text and does not capture or store microphone audio. Availability, languages and processing follow system settings and Apple's policies. The app has no advertising or analytics SDK, tracking, or app sign-up system.

Choices and support

Stop receiving, disable wireless, rotate pairing codes or revoke permissions in system settings. Uninstalling removes the app container; Keychain entries may remain. It does not promise erasure of all Keychain entries or a fresh trial. Mac Launch at Login can be disabled in the app or system Login Items.

If you voluntarily email support, the company receives your message and supplied contact details to address your request. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or unrelated desktop content. Website servers may keep normal access logs needed to deliver pages and operate them securely; these static product pages load no advertising or analytics scripts. This notice will be updated when relevant practices or features change.
