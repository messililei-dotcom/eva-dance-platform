两端打开程序，连接 USB 或同一本地网络，允许所需权限。断线后点蓝色悬浮刷新按钮，或设置第一项“刷新连接”。Mac 配套端需保持运行。
Open both apps, connect USB or the same local network, and grant required permissions. Use the floating blue Refresh button or the first Settings action to reconnect. Keep the Mac companion running.
